About FairSpin

The fair name wheel.

What it does

FairSpin is a bright, 3D name wheel for raffles, giveaways, classroom picks, team stand-ups and deciding who goes first. Paste your list of names, spin, and let the wheel decide. With Fair mode on, everyone gets picked once before anyone repeats.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste names into the list — one name per line (you can paste a whole column from Excel or Sheets).
  2. Turn on Fair mode if everyone should be picked once before anyone repeats.
  3. Click Spin, or press Space / Ctrl + Enter.
  4. See every pick in the Results tab. Shuffle, sort, remove duplicates or clear the list anytime.

Why I made this

I don't trust most “free” tools online.

Most free websites that convert a PDF or crunch your numbers do it on their own servers. Your files and data leave your device, and you have to trust that they're deleted afterwards. Very few of them can honestly promise zero data retention.

I use these every day — so I made my own.

These are tools I actually need in my day-to-day work and life. Instead of uploading my stuff to a stranger's server every time, I built small versions that run entirely in the browser. Nothing to upload, nothing to retain.

Made for me, shared with you. Why not?

I built them for personal use, but there's no reason to keep them to myself. If they're useful to me, they might be useful to you too — free, no sign-up, and your data stays with you.

Frequently asked questions

Is the wheel really random?

Yes. Each spin uses your browser's random number generator, and every slice has an equal chance unless you add a name more than once.

What does Fair mode do?

It removes a name after it's picked, so nobody is chosen twice until everyone has had a turn.

Are my names saved?

Your list is kept only in this browser so it's still there next time. It is never uploaded.

Your privacy, in short

FairSpin does its work in your browser. Your list of names and settings are saved in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time. They never leave your device; clear them with the Clear button or your browser settings. Read the full Privacy Policy.

FairSpin is for fun and informal picks. It is not intended for regulated lotteries, gambling or contests that legally require certified random drawing.
